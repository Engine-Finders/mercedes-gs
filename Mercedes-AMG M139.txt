## About the Mercedes-AMG M139 Engine

The M139 is the most powerful four-cylinder engine ever offered from the factory, developed by Mercedes-AMG. In S guise, it produces 421 hp from just 2.0 litres. It's the heart of the current-generation 45-series AMG models (A 45, CLA 45, GLA 45) and has more recently been adopted for the 43-series models (C 43, GLC 43) and the entry-level SL 43.

## Mercedes-AMG M139 Applications by Model

The M139 is deployed across the **A-Class** (A 45 AMG / A 45 S AMG, W177), the **CLA** (CLA 45 AMG / CLA 45 S AMG, C118), and the **GLA-Class** (GLA 45 AMG / GLA 45 S AMG, H247). These form the core of the high-performance M139 range. More recently, the engine has been adopted for the **C-Class** (C 43 AMG, W206), the **GLC-Class** (GLC 43 AMG, X254), and the **SL** roadster (SL 43, R232), proving the M139's versatility across different vehicle types.

## Mercedes-AMG M139 Engine Compatibility

Because the M139 is fitted to six different models — from the compact A-Class to the grand-touring SL roadster — compatibility comes down to confirming your exact model, generation, and specific AMG variant (e.g., "45" vs "45 S" vs "43"). A M139 from an A 45 AMG isn't a drop-in match for a M139 from an SL 43 — ancillaries and mounting differ. We confirm by registration/VIN before quoting or supplying anything.
