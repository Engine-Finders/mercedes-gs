## Why We Rebuild Instead of Swap

Most engine suppliers sell you a used engine of unknown history and hope it matches. We do it differently.

We rebuild the engine your C-Class already has — strip it down, pressure-test it, and replace every worn or damaged component with new OEM parts. You get a near-new engine that's better than what came out, with a 12-month unlimited-mileage warranty.
